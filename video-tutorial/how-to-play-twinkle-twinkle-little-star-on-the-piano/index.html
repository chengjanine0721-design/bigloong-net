How to Play Twinkle Twinkle Little Star on the Piano

By Alice Baker

Twinkle Twinkle Little Star is one of the first songs nearly every piano learner meets. It is an easy, beginner-level piece: the melody uses only a few notes, stays in a comfortable range, and repeats a simple pattern, so it suits complete beginners and young children well. Most learners can play the right-hand tune in a single afternoon and put both hands together within a week of short, regular practice. Its slow, familiar tune makes it ideal for building note reading, steady rhythm and confidence at the keyboard before moving on to more demanding pieces.

What you will learn

  1. Find middle C, then locate the notes C C G G A A G using the group of two black keys as your guide.
  2. Play the opening phrase one note at a time and say each note name aloud: C C G G A A G, then hold the G for two beats.
  3. Learn the second phrase F F E E D D C and listen for the way it steps down back to the starting note.
  4. Keep the melody in the right hand with fingers 1 and 5 on the repeated C and G notes, using finger 3 for the A.
  5. Add a simple left hand by playing a low C on the first beat of each bar, then try alternating low C and G for a fuller sound.
  6. Put both hands together slowly with a metronome, and only increase the tempo once the notes are even and relaxed.
Tip Practise one step at a time and only move on when the current one feels easy.
