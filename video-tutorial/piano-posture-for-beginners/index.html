Piano Posture for Beginners

By Alice Baker
Sit Position
Hand Position
Incorrect Posture
Correct Posture

Sit tall, relax your shoulders and find the right bench height. Good posture prevents tension and makes every note easier to play.

What you will learn

  1. Place the bench so your forearms are level with the keyboard when your fingers rest on the keys.
  2. Sit on the front half of the bench with feet flat on the floor and a straight but relaxed back.
  3. Keep elbows slightly away from your body and let your shoulders drop.
  4. Curve your fingers naturally and play from the weight of your arm rather than finger force alone.
Tip Practise one step at a time and only move on when the current one feels easy.
