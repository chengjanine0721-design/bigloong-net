How to Play Erika on Piano

By Alice Baker
Erika Piano (Easy Version)

A short, easy-to-follow piano version you can play along with.

Learn how to play Erika on piano with a simple melody in the right hand, easy left hand chords and a steady march-like rhythm, ideal for beginners.

What you will learn

  1. Erika uses a simple three-chord pattern. In G major the main chords are G (G B D), C (C E G) and D (D F sharp A). Find each shape and practise moving between them with your left hand.
  2. Learn the right hand melody in small phrases. The opening notes are C, then D E E E A A, so place your right thumb on C and use fingers 1 2 3 for the stepwise movement.
  3. Break the tune into two bar chunks and repeat each one slowly until the notes feel automatic before joining the next phrase.
  4. Add a simple left hand by playing the root note of each chord on the first beat of the bar: G, C and D. Keep it low and steady under the melody.
  5. Practise the melody and bass hands separately first. Once each is comfortable, put them together at a slow tempo with a metronome.
  6. Keep an even, march-like rhythm and a firm but relaxed touch. Increase the speed only when the notes and chord changes are clean and even.
Tip Practise one step at a time and only move on when the current one feels easy.
