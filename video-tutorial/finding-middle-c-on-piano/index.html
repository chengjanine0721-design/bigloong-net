Finding Middle C on a Piano

By Alice Baker
▶ Video tutorial coming soon

Middle C is your home base on the keyboard. Learn two quick ways to find it every time you sit down to play.

What you will learn

  1. Look for the group of two black keys near the centre of the keyboard.
  2. The white key immediately to the left of that pair is middle C.
  3. On a full 88 key piano, middle C is also the fourth C counted up from the lowest note.
  4. Mark it once with a removable sticker if you are new, then remove it as your reading improves.
Tip Practise one step at a time and only move on when the current one feels easy.
