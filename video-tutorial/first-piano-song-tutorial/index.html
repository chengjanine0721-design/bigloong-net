Your First Piano Song Tutorial

By Alice Baker
▶Video tutorial coming soon

Put a few notes together in a simple melody you can play in your first week. Slow, even practice is the whole secret.

What you will learn

  1. Learn the right hand melody one phrase at a time, three notes at a time.
  2. Practise slowly with a steady count until the notes feel automatic.
  3. Add the left hand only when the melody is comfortable.
  4. Play hands together at a slow tempo, then gradually increase speed.
Tip Practise one step at a time and only move on when the current one feels easy.
