How to Play Jingle Bells on Piano

By Alice Baker
▶Video tutorial coming soon

Learn how to play Jingle Bells on piano with a simple right-hand melody, easy left-hand chords and clear step-by-step practice for beginners.

What you will learn

  1. Find the starting note first. Most easy arrangements of Jingle Bells sit in C major, so place your right thumb on middle C. The melody stays within a small range around C, which makes it a good first song for new players.
  2. Learn the opening phrase in four-note groups. The tune begins E E E, E E E, E G C D E. Play each group slowly with fingers 3 3 3, 3 3 3, 3 5 1 2 3, and say the note names aloud until the shape feels familiar.
  3. Practise the second phrase: F F F F, F E E E, E D D E, D G. Keep the rhythm even and count aloud. The repeated notes are easy to rush, so use a metronome set well below the final tempo.
  4. Add the chorus answer phrase: G G G G, G E E E, E D D E, D C. This is the moment the melody comes back home to C. Playing the last note firmly will make the phrase sound complete.
  5. Keep the left hand simple. Play a single low C on the first beat of each bar, then add G as a second root when the melody moves toward the chorus. One note per bar is plenty at first.
  6. Turn the roots into chords once the melody is secure: C major under the verses and G major under the chorus. Play them softly so the right-hand tune stays clearly on top.
  7. Practise hands separately, then put them together two bars at a time. Loop the bars around each change until the hands move without hesitating, and only then increase the tempo.
  8. Play the whole tune from start to finish once a day. Record yourself, listen for uneven repeated notes and fix the hardest phrase first. Smooth, steady playing sounds better than rushing.
Tip Practise one step at a time and only move on when the current one feels easy.
