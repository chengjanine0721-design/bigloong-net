Privacy Policy

How Bigloong handles the small amount of information involved in using this site.

Information we collect

Bigloong does not require an account and does not ask for personal details to read guides or watch tutorials. If you contact us by email, we receive the message you choose to send.

Cookies and analytics

We use basic analytics to understand which guides are helpful. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

Email and forms

If you write to us, your message is handled only to reply to your question. We do not sell or share your email address.

Your choices

You can browse the entire site without sharing information. For any question about your data, contact us at contact@bigloong.net and we will help.
