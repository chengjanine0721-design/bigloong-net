About Us

Bigloong is a small independent site for people who love the piano.

Our story

Bigloong started with a simple idea. Finding a clear piano guide should not take longer than learning the piece itself. What began as notes for friends grew into a collection of practical guides.

What we publish

We write in depth guides, short video tutorials and quick answers that help you learn and buy with confidence. Every piece is checked against real playing experience and updated when needed.

Who writes here

Guides are written by Alice Baker with care for accuracy and readability. We explain our reasoning so you can apply it to your own playing.

Get in touch

Questions, suggestions or a topic you would like us to cover. Email us at contact@bigloong.net and we will get back to you as soon as we can.
